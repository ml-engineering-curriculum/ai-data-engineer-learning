## Overview

This track teaches the *engineering craft* of the AI training and evaluation data pipeline end-to-end for a hands-on specialist at level 30: reading AI data-governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF + GenAI Profile, ISO/IEC 42001 clause 8, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 5259 series, ISO/IEC 25012, EU AI Act Articles 10 + 53 + Annex XI, GDPR Articles 5/6/9/14/22, CCPA/CPRA, EU DSM Article 4 TDM-exception, FDA GMLP / PCCP, SR 11-7, Colorado AI Act) as an engineer would → sourcing data defensibly (license matrix, robots.txt per RFC 9309, ai.txt / llms.txt / 'noai' / GPTBot / CCBot / ClaudeBot / Google-Extended opt-outs, EU DSM Article 4 TDM reservations, C2PA Content Credentials) → running web-scale acquisition and curation pipelines (Common Crawl, CCNet, trafilatura / resiliparse, FastText language-id, boilerplate stripping) with the public reference pipelines (Dolma, FineWeb / FineWeb-Edu, RedPajama-Data v2, RefinedWeb) → measuring data quality against ISO/IEC 25012 and ISO/IEC 5259 with Great Expectations, Soda, Deequ, and pandera and running Dodge-style corpus audits → deduplicating at trillion-token scale (MinHash + LSH per Broder 1997, datasketch, Alibaba Data-Juicer, NVIDIA NeMo Curator, dolma dedup) with the Lee 2021 and Kandpal 2022 memorisation + privacy gains defended → engineering benchmark-contamination firewalls (defending against Min-K% Prob per Shi 2023 and the Oren 2023 exchangeability test, running the lm-eval-harness decontamination recipes) → operating human-annotation programs (Label Studio, Argilla, Prodigy, Doccano, vendor-RFP + vendor-audit shape for Scale AI / Surge AI / Appen / Sama / iMerit / Toloka, Krippendorff's alpha + Cohen's / Fleiss' kappa, Settles active learning, Snorkel weak supervision, RLHF / DPO preference labelling) → generating synthetic data at production scale (Self-Instruct, Evol-Instruct, UltraChat, teacher-student distillation, DPO preference-pair synthesis, Shumailov 2024 'Curse of Recursion' bounding, privacy-preserving tabular synth with SDV / Gretel / MOSTLY AI) → versioning + lineage + reproducibility (DVC, lakeFS, Pachyderm, Delta Lake / Iceberg + Nessie, OpenLineage + Marquez, DataHub, Databricks Unity Catalog, MLCommons Croissant metadata, CycloneDX ML-BOM, SPDX AI profile, SLSA + Sigstore signing) → PII detection + redaction + privacy risk (Microsoft Presidio, scrubadub, Google Cloud DLP, AWS Comprehend PII, defending Carlini 2020/2022 extraction attacks) → data-quality monitoring and regression detection in production (whylogs / Evidently / Monte Carlo / Soda Cloud / Metaplane / Bigeye) → owning a cross-functional data program at team scope with dataset cards (Hugging Face / Google / Meta formats), Datasheets for Datasets end-to-end, EU AI Act Article 53 + Annex XI training-data summary authoring for GPAI providers, and the data-slice of model / system cards.

It is a **hands-on engineering specialist track at level 30**, positioned in the **ML Engineering family**, as a peer to `fine-tuning-engineer` (level 30, SFT-dataset-design depth), `rag-engineer` (level 30, retrieval-side ingestion depth), `model-evaluation-engineer` (level 30, benchmark-construction depth), `nlp-engineer` (level 30, tokeniser-training depth), and `ai-eval-engineer` (level 30, AI Engineering family, application-trace depth). The differentiator is *training-data engineering ownership*: this curriculum sources, licenses, acquires, curates, deduplicates, decontaminates, labels, synthesises, versions, PII-scrubs, and quality-monitors the datasets the modeling peers consume.

Total planned commitment: **166 hours across 12 modules** + **120 hours across 3 projects** = **~286 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the engineering craft of the AI training / evaluation data pipeline end-to-end — AI data-governance framework fluency, data sourcing / licensing / consent / provenance, web-scale acquisition + curation, data-quality measurement + audit, deduplication at scale, benchmark contamination detection + decontamination, labelling operations + annotation programs, synthetic data generation at production scale, dataset versioning + lineage + reproducibility, PII detection + redaction + privacy risk, data-quality monitoring + regression detection, and a cross-functional data program at team scope.
- **Defers down** to [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical data-engineering fundamentals (pandas, SQL, Parquet, basic DVC, feature-store consumer tier) and classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / MLflow fundamentals; to [`ai-governance-analyst-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for operational analyst legwork this role consumes; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
- **Defers sideways** to [`fine-tuning-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/fine-tuning-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for SFT dataset design depth (dataset-mix cards, preference-pair curation, DPO/ORPO pair generation) — this curriculum supplies the raw sourced + curated + deduplicated + decontaminated corpus; to [`rag-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for retrieval-side chunking + embeddings + vector DBs + hybrid retrieval depth — this curriculum supplies the clean corpus; to [`model-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for gold-standard eval-set labelling methodology + judge-vs-human calibration + canary-set design depth — this curriculum covers production labelling ops; to [`nlp-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/nlp-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for tokeniser-training depth — this curriculum supplies the pretokenisation corpus; to [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-trace / judge / online-eval depth; to [`ai-infra-ml-platform-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-ml-platform-learning) (peer, level 30, Infra family) for self-service ML platform engineering; to [`ai-infra-mlops-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation + orchestrator platform; to [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (peer, level 25, Governance family) for harm-model / adversarial-ML / guardrail depth.
- **Defers up** to [`training-pipeline-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/training-pipeline-engineer-learning) (level 35) for cluster-side ingestion depth (WebDataset / Mosaic Streaming / Ray Data at shard scale, tokenisation throughput, I/O pipelines) — this curriculum designs the pipeline shape and hand-offs; to [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (level 35) for deep ML / AI security at platform scale; to [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth; to [`staff-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/staff-ml-engineer-learning) (level 40) for foundation-model data-mixture strategy at org scope; to [`principal-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/principal-ml-engineer-learning) (level 48), [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60), and [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for strategic / program / executive scope.
- **Out of scope** to legal counsel (this role does not deliver legal opinion on copyright, licensing, or jurisdictional data law — it packages the artifacts legal reviews) and to SOC / DFIR (data-breach incident handling).

See [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements-to-coverage map and the cited public references the catalog is grounded in.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — AI Training-Data Governance Frameworks and the Role Position
Read NIST AI RMF 1.0 (with the Playbook and GenAI Profile) as an engineer — mapping every MEASURE / MANAGE sub-category that lands on training-data integrity, provenance, representativeness, bias, and documentation to a concrete pipeline artifact. Read ISO/IEC 42001 clause 8 (operational data controls), ISO/IEC 23894 (risk sources in data), ISO/IEC 5259 series (Data quality for AI/ML), ISO/IEC 25012 (data-quality model). Read EU AI Act Articles 10 (data governance for high-risk systems), 53 + Annex XI (GPAI training-data summary), and 72 (post-market monitoring, data-side signals), with the EU AI Office GPAI Code of Practice as the voluntary code anticipating regulator expectations. Layer GDPR Articles 5/6/9/14/22, CCPA/CPRA, and EU DSM Article 4 TDM-exception + opt-out. Position SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12 / FDA GMLP + PCCP / Colorado AI Act as sector / geography flavours. Diagram this role's exact scope on the level ladder — what `ml-engineer` (level 20) already owns on the data-engineering substrate, what `fine-tuning-engineer` / `rag-engineer` / `model-evaluation-engineer` / `nlp-engineer` (peers, level 30) do on top, and what `training-pipeline-engineer` (level 35) and `staff-ml-engineer` (level 40) inherit upward.

### mod-102 — Data Sourcing, Licensing, Consent, and Provenance Engineering
Catalog data-source types (public web crawl, licensed partner, user-generated, third-party datasets on Hugging Face Hub / Kaggle / Zenodo, synthetic) and author a defensible selection matrix per training objective. Author a data-license matrix (Creative Commons tier mapping, proprietary licenses, commercial-use carve-outs, allowlist / denylist). Honour crawling and training opt-out contracts — robots.txt per RFC 9309, ai.txt / llms.txt / 'noai' / Cloudflare AI-opt-out / Google-Extended / GPTBot / CCBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot, IETF ai-pref working group's standards-track evolution — and the EU DSM Article 4 TDM-exception opt-out. Emit per-source Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru 2018), Data Statements for NLP (Bender & Friedman 2018), Dataset Nutrition Labels (Holland 2018), Croissant (MLCommons), and Hugging Face Dataset Card YAML metadata. Attach CycloneDX ML-BOM + SPDX 3.0 AI-profile + C2PA Content Credentials where upstream publishers provide them. Coordinate with `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) on DPIA, `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) on data-side harm modelling, and `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) on the regulator-facing package.

### mod-103 — Web-Scale Data Acquisition and Curation Pipelines
Pull a non-trivial Common Crawl slice and run the CCNet-style pipeline (Wenzek et al. 2019) end-to-end — language-id with FastText lid.176, perplexity filter with KenLM-style model, dedup — producing a documented reproducible slice. Run HTML main-content extraction with trafilatura (benchmarked against resiliparse) against the C4 / RefinedWeb methodology. Reproduce a scoped subset of a public reference pipeline — Dolma (Soldaini 2024), RedPajama-Data v2 (quality-signal-per-document), FineWeb / FineWeb-Edu (Penedo et al. 2024), The Pile (Gao 2020). Use a working operator library — Alibaba Data-Juicer, NVIDIA NeMo Curator, Dolma toolkit, Ray Data — not a hand-rolled one. Keep the crawling-ethics contract from mod-102 enforced in code. Hand off to `training-pipeline-engineer` (level 35) on cluster-side sharding + tokenisation throughput.

### mod-104 — Data Quality Measurement, Representativeness, and Corpus Audit
Author Great Expectations suites, Soda Core checks, AWS Deequ (Spark) rules, and pandera dataframe schemas exercising the ISO/IEC 25012 quality characteristics and the ISO/IEC 5259-2 quality measures for AI/ML. Run a Dodge-style audit on a sourced slice — what filters strip, demographic representation of the filtered-out content, contamination against public benchmarks. Apply Kreutzer 2022 'Quality at a Glance' to multilingual slices. Run DataComp / DataComp-LM methodology for data-centric ablations (fixed model, variable data) to prove which filter contributes which eval delta. Emit a per-slice quality report that plugs into the dataset card from mod-112 and the data-mixture-strategy work at `staff-ml-engineer` (level 40).

### mod-105 — Deduplication at Trillion-Token Scale
Engineer exact dedup (SHA256 / xxhash) and near-dup (MinHash + LSH per Broder 1997, SimHash) pipelines at document, paragraph, and sentence granularity. Scale with datasketch, data-juicer, NeMo Curator, dolma dedup. Reason about hash-count × band-count × row-count trade-offs and target Jaccard thresholds. Defend the perplexity, memorisation, and privacy gains from Lee et al. 2021, Kandpal et al. 2022, and the SlimPajama dedup report — dedup is a load-bearing quality + privacy control, not operational hygiene. Detect public data-poisoning tools (Nightshade / Glaze) and adversarial perturbations, mapping to MITRE ATLAS and OWASP ML Top 10 ML02 / OWASP LLM Top 10 LLM03; coordinate adversarial-ML methodology depth with `ai-risk-engineer` (peer, level 25). Package the dedup report as audit evidence for mod-110 and mod-112.

### mod-106 — Benchmark Contamination Detection and Decontamination Engineering
Author the contamination firewall — the pipeline step that keeps MMLU / GSM8K / HumanEval / HELM / BigBench / MATH / LiveBench / LiveCodeBench test sets out of pretrain + SFT + preference-pair generation corpora — using n-gram overlap, exact-prompt matching, and LLM-based substring detectors. Defend against the Shi et al. 2023 Min-K% Prob detector and the Oren et al. 2023 exchangeability test. Run the EleutherAI LM Evaluation Harness decontamination recipes and compare to Dolma practice. Handle the recursive-synthetic-data contamination vector (coordinate with mod-108). Hand off benchmark-construction and gold-eval-set-design depth to `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30).

### mod-107 — Labelling Operations, Annotation Programs, and Preference Labelling
Run production annotation programs in Label Studio, Argilla, Prodigy, and doccano. Contract and audit vendor labelling (Scale AI, Surge AI, Appen, Sama, iMerit, Toloka, SuperAnnotate) — vendor-RFP shape, written labelling guidelines, calibration set, per-item QA, vendor-side SLA, escalation + arbitration. Compute inter-annotator agreement correctly — Cohen's kappa, Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha. Budget labelling with Settles 2009 active learning. Amplify human labels with Snorkel weak supervision + data programming (Ratner 2016). Run preference-labelling programs for RLHF / DPO / ORPO (pairwise UI, Likert / fine-grained rubric, calibration drift, chatbot-arena-style protocols). Coordinate with `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30) on gold-eval-set methodology; hand off preference-pair curation depth to `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, level 30).

### mod-108 — Synthetic Data Generation at Production Scale
Author Self-Instruct (Wang 2022), Evol-Instruct / WizardLM (Xu 2023), and UltraChat-style (Ding 2023) generation programs. Run teacher-to-student distillation with license propagation reasoning. Engineer preference-pair synthesis for DPO / ORPO / KTO / RLHF; hand off SFT-side preference curation to `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, level 30). Defend against the Shumailov et al. 2024 'Curse of Recursion' failure mode — human anchoring, iteration budgets, quality filters. Use NVIDIA NeMo Curator + Alibaba Data-Juicer synth operators at scale. Engineer privacy-preserving synthetic data for structured data with SDV, Gretel AI, MOSTLY AI, with DP guarantees on the generator. Keep the contamination firewall from mod-106 enforced on synthetic generation.

### mod-109 — Dataset Versioning, Lineage, and Reproducibility
Version non-trivial datasets with DVC, lakeFS, and Pachyderm. Version large tables with Delta Lake and Apache Iceberg (+ Nessie) when the substrate is a lakehouse. Track lineage with OpenLineage (open protocol), Marquez (reference server), DataHub, and Databricks Unity Catalog — authoring side (event emission) and consuming side (querying lineage before a dataset change). Sign dataset snapshots with Sigstore; emit SLSA level-1+ provenance attestations; produce CycloneDX ML-BOM + SPDX AI-profile records per snapshot. Publish Croissant (MLCommons), Hugging Face Dataset Card YAML, and FAIR-principles metadata. Hand off cluster-side storage layouts — WebDataset tar-shards, MosaicML StreamingDataset format, Hugging Face Datasets Parquet + Arrow, Ray Data streaming — to `training-pipeline-engineer` (level 35) with a documented hand-off contract.

### mod-110 — PII Detection, Redaction, and Privacy Risk for Training Data
Engineer PII detection + redaction pipelines with Microsoft Presidio (recognisers, context-aware analysis), scrubadub, Google Cloud Sensitive Data Protection (DLP), and AWS Comprehend PII — combining regex, dictionary, and ML-based recognisers. Choose redaction primitives per use case — masking, tokenisation, surrogate generation, format-preserving redaction, hashing. Reason about lawful basis under GDPR Articles 6 + 9 + 14, CCPA / CPRA 'sale or sharing', and HIPAA de-identification (safe-harbor + expert-determination) — routing legal opinion to counsel. Defend against the Carlini et al. 2020 'Extracting Training Data from LLMs' and Carlini 2022 'Quantifying Memorization' attacks with pipeline-side controls. Reason about the Kandpal et al. 2022 dedup-privacy linkage. Hand off DP-SGD training depth to `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, level 30) and `ai-risk-engineer` (peer, level 25).

### mod-111 — Data Quality Monitoring and Regression Detection
Design the dataset-side monitoring contract — which metric feeds which alert, at what cadence, routing to which owner — distinct from model-output monitoring and distinct from classical data-warehouse monitoring. Use whylogs, Evidently AI, Monte Carlo, Soda Cloud, Metaplane, and Bigeye. Measure data drift, schema drift, and population drift on recurring refreshes; attach alerts to the dataset version-pin. Author regression fixtures — minimal reproducible slices that re-run on every pipeline change. Hand off orchestrator / scheduler engineering to `ai-infra-mlops` (peer, level 25) and model-output / online-eval depth to `ai-eval-engineer` (peer, level 30) and `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30).

### mod-112 — Cross-Functional Data Program, Dataset Cards, and Communication
Own an AI training-data program at team scope across ML / infra / security / governance / legal — cadence, artifacts, decisions, sign-off routing. Brief the AI governance council and the data council with pre-read decks, one-pagers, and decision-log templates. Author dataset cards end-to-end in the Hugging Face, Google, and Meta formats. Draft the EU AI Act Article 53 + Annex XI training-data summary for a general-purpose AI provider. Contribute the data slice to a model card (Mitchell et al. 2019 / Hugging Face model card format) and the dataset slice to a system card. Coordinate with `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) on impact-assessment legwork, with `ai-risk-engineer` (peer, level 25) on the risk-register slice, with `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) on release-assurance, and with `staff-ml-engineer` (level 40) on data-mixture strategy. Position DAMA CDMP, IAPP CIPP / AIGP, and ISO/IEC 42001 lead-implementer in the certifications portfolio.

## Assessment

Each module ships **1 quiz** plus the exercises and a lab listed in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Each project ships a portfolio-grade README, a reproducibility bundle (pipeline code, pinned source URLs with observed SHA256 of downloaded shards, dedup + contamination + PII-scrub reports, lineage events exported from OpenLineage, Sigstore signatures, Croissant + ML-BOM + Dataset Card metadata), and an explicit rubric covering sourcing + licensing defensibility, acquisition + curation reproducibility, quality-audit depth, dedup rigour, contamination-firewall completeness, labelling-ops quality (IAA + vendor-audit + weak-sup amplifier), synthetic-data firewall + recursion-bound, versioning + lineage + reproducibility, PII-scrub rigour, data-quality monitoring + regression fixtures, and cross-audience communication.
