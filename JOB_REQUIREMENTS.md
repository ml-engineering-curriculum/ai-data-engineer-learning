## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Data Engineer (level 30, ML Engineering family) from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`ml-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical data-engineering fundamentals (pandas, SQL, Parquet, basic DVC usage, feature-store consumer tier) and classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / MLflow fundamentals.
   - *Down* to [`ai-governance-analyst`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for operational analyst legwork (AI-system inventory, jurisdictional tracking, first-draft impact assessments) this role consumes.
   - *Down* to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites (Linux, Git, Python, packaging, HTTP APIs).
   - *Sideways* to [`fine-tuning-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/fine-tuning-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for SFT dataset design depth (dataset mix cards, preference-pair curation, DPO/ORPO pair generation) — the AI Data Engineer supplies the raw sourced + curated + deduplicated + decontaminated corpus; that peer shapes the task-specific SFT slice.
   - *Sideways* to [`rag-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for retrieval-side chunking / embeddings / vector DBs / hybrid retrieval depth — the AI Data Engineer supplies the clean corpus; the RAG engineer designs the retrieval contract on top.
   - *Sideways* to [`model-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for gold-standard eval-set labelling methodology, judge-vs-human calibration, canary-set design depth — this role covers production labelling ops; benchmark construction depth is linked out.
   - *Sideways* to [`nlp-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/nlp-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30) for tokeniser-training depth (SentencePiece / BPE on domain-adapted corpora, vocab design).
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-eval-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer trace / judge / eval depth.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-infra-ml-platform`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-ml-platform-learning) (peer, level 30, Infra family) for self-service ML platform engineering (feature store, dataset registry, lineage-platform runtime).
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-infra-mlops`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps pipeline orchestration and CI/CD-for-ML platform engineering.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-risk-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (peer, level 25, Governance family) for harm-model / adversarial-ML / guardrail depth.
   - *Up* to [`training-pipeline-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/training-pipeline-engineer-learning) (level 35) for cluster-side ingestion depth (WebDataset / Mosaic Streaming / Ray Data at shard scale, tokenisation throughput, I/O pipelines) — this role designs the pipeline and hand-off; that peer implements the cluster execution.
   - *Up* to [`ai-infra-security`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (level 35) for deep ML / AI security at platform scale.
   - *Up* to [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth.
   - *Up* to [`staff-ml-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/staff-ml-engineer-learning) (level 40) for foundation-model data-mixture strategy at org scope.
   - *Up* to [`principal-ml-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/principal-ml-engineer-learning) (level 48) for org and industry strategy; to [`head-of-ai-governance`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60) and [`chief-ai-officer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for program leadership.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel (this role does not deliver legal opinion on copyright, licensing, or jurisdictional data law — it packages the artifacts legal reviews) and to SOC / DFIR (data-breach incident handling).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty.

- **ML Engineer** (level 20, `ml-engineer-learning`) — owns classical data-engineering fundamentals (pandas, SQL, Parquet, basic DVC, feature-store consumer tier) and the classical-ML stack. **This AI Data Engineer assumes those and does not re-teach them.**
- **AI Governance Analyst** (level 15, `ai-governance-analyst-learning`) — owns operational analyst work (inventory, DPIA, framework crosswalks, impact assessments). **This role consumes those artifacts.**
- **AI Risk Engineer** (peer, level 25, Governance family, `ai-risk-engineer-learning`) — owns the engineering craft of AI risk (harm modelling, adversarial-ML, red-team, guardrail, privacy-risk engineering). **This role supplies the dataset provenance, contamination report, PII-scrub log, and dataset card that peer consumes.**
- **AI Infra MLOps** (peer, level 25, `ai-infra-mlops-learning`) — owns MLOps automation and orchestrator platforms. **This role authors the data-pipeline shape; that peer runs the scheduler.**
- **AI Data Engineer** (this track, level 30, ML Engineering family) — owns the engineering craft of the training / evaluation data pipeline (sourcing, licensing, curation, dedup, decontamination, labelling ops, synthetic generation, versioning + lineage, PII scrub, quality monitoring, dataset-card authoring).
- **Fine-Tuning Engineer** (peer, level 30, `fine-tuning-engineer-learning`) — owns SFT dataset design, preference-pair curation, DPO/ORPO pair generation. **This role supplies the raw corpus; that peer shapes the SFT slice.**
- **RAG Engineer** (peer, level 30, `rag-engineer-learning`) — owns retrieval-side chunking / embeddings / vector DBs / hybrid retrieval. **This role supplies the clean corpus; that peer builds the retrieval contract.**
- **Model Evaluation Engineer** (peer, level 30, `model-evaluation-engineer-learning`) — owns gold-standard eval-set labelling methodology, judge-vs-human calibration, canary-set design. **This role supplies the data-ops labelling platform; that peer supplies the eval-methodology shape.**
- **NLP Engineer** (peer, level 30, `nlp-engineer-learning`) — owns tokeniser training depth. **This role supplies the pretokenisation corpus.**
- **AI Eval Engineer** (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family, `ai-eval-engineer-learning`) — owns application-trace / judge / online-eval depth. **This role supplies training-data provenance that eval records reference for fair-eval claims.**
- **AI Infra ML Platform** (peer, level 30, `ai-infra-ml-platform-learning`) — owns self-service ML platform. **This role plugs into that platform.**
- **Training Pipeline Engineer** (next-up, level 35, `training-pipeline-engineer-learning`) — owns cluster-side ingestion (WebDataset / Mosaic Streaming / Ray Data at shard scale, tokenisation throughput). **This role designs the pipeline and hand-off shape; that peer implements the cluster execution.**
- **AI Infra Security** (next-up, level 35, `ai-infra-security-learning`) — owns deep ML / AI security at platform scale.
- **AI Evaluation Engineer** (next-up, level 35, Governance family, `ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`) — owns release-assurance methodology. **This role supplies the dataset-side evidence (datasheets, provenance, licensing, PII-scrub, contamination log).**
- **Staff ML Engineer** (next-up, level 40, `staff-ml-engineer-learning`) — owns foundation-model data-mixture strategy. **This role supplies per-source datasets and licensing contracts; that peer sequences the mixture.**
- **Principal ML Engineer** (level 48) / **Head of AI Governance** (level 60) / **Chief AI Officer** (level 70) — leadership scope.
