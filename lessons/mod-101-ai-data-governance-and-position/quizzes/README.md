# Quizzes for mod-101-ai-data-governance-and-position

Knowledge checks for the module. Each quiz tests whether you can *use* the frameworks as an engineer — translate a clause into a pipeline artifact, pick the right regime for a scenario, route a request to the right role — rather than recite wording.

- [Quiz 01 — Governance Frameworks and the AI Data Engineer Role Scope](quiz-01-governance-frameworks-and-role-scope.md) — 10 multiple-choice questions spanning Chapters 01–06 (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 / 23894 / 25012 / 5259, EU AI Act Articles 10 / 53 / 72, GDPR / CCPA / DSM Article 4, SR 11-7 / FDA / Colorado AI Act, and the role's scope on the level ladder).
