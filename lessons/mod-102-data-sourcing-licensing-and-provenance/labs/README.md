# Labs for mod-102-data-sourcing-licensing-and-provenance (Data Sourcing, Licensing, Consent, and Provenance Engineering)

Guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions for the module. Each lab has a stated duration, prerequisites, numbered steps with commands or code, expected output, and checkpoints. Labs are *guided practice*; they differ from the module's `exercises/`, which are open-ended design work.

- [Lab 01 — Assemble the Chapter 07 Handoff Bundle for One Slice](lab-01-assemble-the-chapter-07-handoff-bundle.md) — 90 min. Compose the three Chapter 07 peer-handoff YAMLs (`dpia_input_bundle.yaml`, `data_risk_register.yaml`, `article_53_1_d_summary_input.yaml`) for a single fixture slice, write a cross-bundle validator that asserts `slice_id` consistency and source-type composition agreement across handoffs 2 and 3, and watch the validator fail on purpose then pass again after a fix. Based on Chapter 07, with vocabulary from Chapters 01, 02, 05, 06.
- [Lab 02 — Implement and Test the Composite Admit Function](lab-02-implement-the-composite-admit-function.md) — 90 min. Implement the Chapter 04 §"How Article 4 and the signals from Chapter 03 compose" `composite_admit` function in runnable Python, stub the four child gates (licence, opt-out, privacy, jurisdiction) so the composition is isolatable under test, write pytest coverage for one admit per Chapter 01 source type, one deny per child-gate, the `ADMIT_WITH_CONDITION`-is-admission rule, the `posture_in_force` recording rule, and the ordering rule that an opt-out denial stays recorded as an opt-out denial even when privacy also fails, and emit a machine-parseable `decision_log.jsonl` with every sub-decision visible on every line. Based on Chapter 04, with child-gate shapes from Chapters 02 and 03.
