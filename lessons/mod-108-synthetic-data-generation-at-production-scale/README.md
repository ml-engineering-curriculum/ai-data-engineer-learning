# mod-108 — Synthetic Data Generation at Production Scale

> **Role:** AI Data Engineer · **Family:** ML Engineering · **Level:** L30

**Estimated effort:** 18 hours

## What this module is about

[`mod-107`](../mod-107-labeling-operations-and-annotation-programs/README.md) stood up the human-labelling program. This module stands up the one piece of the pipeline that **creates training data without humans in the inner loop**: synthetic generation. Done well, it multiplies the labelled corpus by 10×–1000× at a fraction of the per-item cost. Done badly, it bakes model collapse (Shumailov et al. 2024), teacher-license violations, benchmark contamination, and silent quality drift directly into the training data.

The deliverable at the end of the module is a **synthetic-data generation package** for a real slice:

1. **Generation program manifest** — seeds, prompts, generator model identity, generation settings, iteration budget, run SHAs.
2. **Diversity + quality filter pipeline** — dedup against seeds, ROUGE-L / embedding similarity thresholds, judge-model score thresholds, length and language filters.
3. **Preference-pair artifact** (where the program produces DPO / ORPO / KTO input) — paired generations, judge verdicts, pair-balance report, difficulty-stratification bucket counts.
4. **Teacher-licence propagation ledger** — teacher model identity, teacher-side licence text SHA, downstream licence decision, counsel sign-off reference.
5. **Curse-of-Recursion defence log** — iteration counter, human-anchor ratio, round-over-round drift metrics, kill-switch threshold.
6. **Contamination firewall report on the generator output** — mod-106 firewall re-run on the synthetic corpus, per-benchmark removal counts, generator-certification lookup.
7. **Tabular-synthesis package** (where the program produces structured data) — generator model (SDV / Gretel / MOSTLY AI), differential-privacy epsilon-delta, utility report, legal-counsel sign-off reference.

By the end you will be able to hand `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, L30) a synthetic SFT or preference-pair corpus that is traceable to seeds, diversity-filtered, judge-scored, teacher-licence-ledgered, firewall-clean, and recursion-budgeted — and defensible to legal, to `ai-risk-engineer`, and to a reader who has read Shumailov 2024.

## Why synthetic generation is an engineering discipline, not a prompt-engineering stunt

Four findings pin the framing. If you take the module as a prompt-engineering exercise you will produce a corpus that reads fine on a 20-item spot-check and reproduces the generator's biases, licence constraints, and benchmark contamination at scale.

- **Wang et al. 2022 (Self-Instruct)** — arXiv 2212.10560. The first production-shaped recipe for bootstrapping an instruction corpus from a tiny seed. The paper's own operating choices — 175 seed tasks, ROUGE-L similarity threshold against the growing instruction pool, exact-filter heuristics — are the baseline every later recipe modifies. Chapters 02 and 08.
- **Xu et al. 2023 (Evol-Instruct / WizardLM)** — arXiv 2304.12244. The depth-and-breadth evolution program that turns a seed pool into a difficulty-stratified corpus. The elimination filter is as load-bearing as the evolution operators. Chapter 03.
- **Shumailov et al. 2024 (*Curse of Recursion* / AI model collapse)** — arXiv 2305.17493 → Nature 631 (2024). Training a model on data generated by (versions of) itself produces measurable distribution collapse. The defence is human-anchoring, iteration budgets, and quality filtering, not more generation. Chapter 07. [[curse-of-recursion-defence]]
- **Rafailov et al. 2023 (DPO)** — arXiv 2305.18290. Direct Preference Optimization is the training-time primitive that consumes paired generations from this module. The pair-synthesis operation is where the preference-signal quality is set, and it cannot be recovered at training time. Chapter 06.

Chapter 01 is the defence of this framing. Do not skip it.

## Learning objectives

- Author **Self-Instruct** (Wang et al. 2022), **Evol-Instruct / WizardLM** (Xu et al. 2023), and **UltraChat-style** (Ding et al. 2023) generation programs — seed prompts, diversity controls, quality filters, deduplication against seeds. Chapters 02, 03, 04.
- Run a **teacher-to-student distillation** program — stronger model generates demonstrations, output is filtered, used to fine-tune a weaker model — and reason about **licensing propagation** (does the teacher licence permit using its outputs for training?). Route legal opinion out of scope to counsel. Chapter 05.
- Engineer **preference-pair synthesis** for DPO / ORPO / KTO / RLHF — paired generations, judge-model scoring, pair balance, difficulty stratification. Hand off the SFT-side preference curation to `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, L30). Chapter 06.
- Defend against the **Shumailov et al. 2024 'Curse of Recursion'** failure mode — iterating a model on its own outputs causes distribution collapse. Use human anchoring, iteration budgets, and quality filters to bound the risk. Chapter 07.
- Use **NVIDIA NeMo Curator** synthetic-data operators and **Alibaba Data-Juicer** synth primitives at scale; do not hand-roll operator libraries. Chapter 08.
- Engineer privacy-preserving synthetic data for tabular / structured data — **Synthetic Data Vault (SDV)**, **Gretel AI**, **MOSTLY AI** — with **differentially-private training of the generator** where regulated-data substitution is the goal. Route legal opinion out of scope to counsel. Chapter 09.
- Keep the **contamination firewall from [`mod-106`](../mod-106-benchmark-contamination-and-decontamination/README.md) enforced on synthetic generation** — the generating model must not have the eval set in its training data, or the contamination is simply laundered into the synthetic corpus. Chapter 10.

## Prerequisites

- You have completed [`mod-101`](../mod-101-ai-data-governance-and-position/README.md) and internalised the ISO/IEC 25012 vocabulary. Synthetic generation is the only pipeline stage that can simultaneously *create* accuracy, *destroy* credibility, and *inherit* contamination.
- You have completed [`mod-104`](../mod-104-data-quality-measurement-and-audit/README.md). The quality filters in this module reuse the per-slice quality metrics defined there.
- You have completed [`mod-105`](../mod-105-deduplication-at-scale/README.md). The dedup-against-seeds step in Self-Instruct and Evol-Instruct reuses the MinHash + LSH infrastructure. ROUGE-L is a replacement for n-gram similarity on short strings, not for document-scale near-duplication.
- You have completed [`mod-106`](../mod-106-benchmark-contamination-and-decontamination/README.md). The synthetic-generator contract pattern (Chapter 05 of mod-106) is the input to Chapter 10 here.
- You have completed [`mod-107`](../mod-107-labeling-operations-and-annotation-programs/README.md) at least through its preference-labelling chapter. Synthetic preference-pair generation is the mechanical analogue of the human preference-pair program.
- Python 3.11+ with: `openai` or `anthropic`, `transformers`, `datasets`, `pyarrow`, `pandas`, `rouge-score`, `sentence-transformers`, `trl` (for DPO formatting), `sdv`, `gretel-synthetics` (or Gretel API key), `nemo-curator`, `data-juicer`. A GPU is helpful for Exercises 04 and 05 and necessary for a non-trivial DPO run; a mid-sized API-model budget substitutes.
- A running local or hosted LLM for the generator role in Exercises 01–05. An OpenAI, Anthropic, or vLLM-served local model all work; the licence implications for each are the Chapter 05 content.

## How to use this module

Chapter 01 is the frame. It is the defence you cite when a product manager asks why we cannot just loop `gpt-5` on itself for a month and ship the output. The four named findings (Wang 2022, Xu 2023, Shumailov 2024, Rafailov 2023) are the citations the role is expected to carry for the rest of the module.

Chapters 02, 03, and 04 are the three canonical generation recipes. Each produces a different shape of corpus — Self-Instruct an instruction-tuning pool, Evol-Instruct a difficulty-stratified pool, UltraChat a multi-turn conversation pool. Exercises 01 and 02 pair with Chapters 02 and 03; UltraChat's topic-tree pattern is treated in prose in Chapter 04 and surfaced as a stretch goal in Exercise 02. In production you will usually combine two or three of the recipes into a single pipeline; the operator-library chapter (Chapter 08) is the place you actually do that.

Chapter 05 is the licensing chapter. It is deliberately short and deliberately conservative: you own the ledger, counsel owns the opinion. Exercise 03 is the hour of pipeline work; writing the ledger shape clearly is what matters.

Chapter 06 is the RLHF / DPO / ORPO / KTO operations chapter — the piece that `fine-tuning-engineer` cares about most. Exercise 04 pairs with it. Keep the hand-off clean: you own the pair-synthesis operation and the judge-model infrastructure; `fine-tuning-engineer` owns the training-time curation.

Chapter 07 is the Curse-of-Recursion defence. Exercise 05 is the ablation that lets you *see* the collapse, on a corpus small enough to iterate in an afternoon. Do not ship a synthetic-data program without having run Exercise 05 at least once in training.

Chapter 08 is the tooling chapter. NeMo Curator and Data-Juicer are the operator libraries you actually use; the first chapters are the recipes you call through them.

Chapter 09 covers the tabular-synthesis slice. It is a separate operating surface — different models (SDV, Gretel, MOSTLY AI), different threat model (membership inference against regulated records), different legal posture (counsel owns the final call). Exercise 06 pairs with it.

Chapter 10 is the firewall chapter. It is the single most-often-skipped step in production synthetic-data programs and the single most common source of silent benchmark-contamination laundering. The firewall contract with [`mod-106`](../mod-106-benchmark-contamination-and-decontamination/README.md) is enforced here.

## Downstream handoffs

- To [`mod-109`](../mod-109-dataset-versioning-lineage-reproducibility/README.md): every synthetic-generation run is a lineage event. The generation manifest, filter report, Curse-of-Recursion log, firewall report, and teacher-licence ledger are first-class provenance records.
- To [`mod-110`](../mod-110-pii-and-privacy-risk-for-training-data/README.md): synthetic data is not automatically PII-free. A generator trained on PII can regurgitate it; a tabular synthesiser without DP can leak membership. The privacy-risk assessment runs on synthetic outputs too.
- To [`mod-111`](../mod-111-data-quality-monitoring-and-regression/README.md): synthetic-corpus quality metrics (dedup rate against seeds, judge-score distribution, length distribution, Curse-of-Recursion KL divergence) are monitored on cadence. A sudden shift between generation runs is a regression signal.
- To [`mod-112`](../mod-112-cross-functional-data-program-and-communication/README.md): the synthetic-data generation package feeds the dataset card — generator identity, seed source, iteration count, filter pass rate, Curse-of-Recursion defence, firewall sign-off.
- To `fine-tuning-engineer` (peer, L30): synthetic SFT corpora, preference pairs, and distillation outputs land here. The hand-off contract is the generation manifest + filter report + firewall report + licence ledger, not raw JSONL.
- To `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, L30): coordinated through [`mod-106`](../mod-106-benchmark-contamination-and-decontamination/README.md). The synthetic-generator contract (Chapter 10) is co-signed with this role when the generator's benchmark coverage is in question.
- To `ai-risk-engineer` (peer, L25): the licence ledger (Chapter 05) and the DP-tabular report (Chapter 09) are joint sign-off artifacts. You own the engineering; they own the risk acceptance.
- To legal counsel (out-of-scope role): you escalate teacher-licence questions (Chapter 05) and regulated-tabular synthesis questions (Chapter 09). You do not render legal opinions in-line.
